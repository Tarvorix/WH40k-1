## 2. Mustering a Boarding Patrol

When mustering a **Terminator Assault** Boarding Patrol:

- All **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** models in your Boarding Patrol must be drawn from the **same Chapter**.
- You can include **up to two of the following units (duplicates are not allowed)**:
  - **Arjac Rockfist**
  - **Belial**
  - **Darnath Lysander**
  - **Logan Grimnar**
  - **Njal Stormcaller**
  - **Ancient in Terminator Armour**
  - **Captain in Terminator Armour**
  - **Chaplain in Terminator Armour**
  - **Librarian in Terminator Armour**
  - **Wolf Guard Battle Leader in Terminator Armour**
  - **Wolf Guard Pack Leader in Terminator Armour**
- You can include **up to two of each** of the following units:
  - **Deathwing Knights** (**5 models**)
  - **Deathwing Terminator Squad** (**5 or 10 models**)
  - **Deathwatch Terminator Squad** (**5 or 10 models**)
  - **Terminator Assault Squad** (**5 or 10 models**)
  - **Terminator Squad** (**5 or 10 models**)

### Boarding Actions size notes

- **Deathwing Knights:** fixed at **5 models** in the mustering entry.
- **Deathwing Terminator Squad / Deathwatch Terminator Squad / Terminator Assault Squad / Terminator Squad:** all may be taken as **5 or 10 models** per the Boarding Actions detachment entry.

## 3. Rules Adaptations

The Terminator Assault Boarding Actions entry lists the following rules adaptations:

- **Terminator Assault Squad** and **Terminator Squad** units can perform the **Secure Site** Tactical Manoeuvre as if they had the **BATTLELINE** keyword.
- All units with the **Teleport Homer** ability lose it.
- **Arjac Rockfist** loses **Champion of the Kingsguard**.
- **Belial** loses **Strikes of Retribution**.
- **Deathwing Knights** lose **Inner Circle**.
- **Logan Grimnar** loses **The Great Wolf**.
- The **Wolf Guard** unit loses **Chosen Companions**.
- **Wolf Guard Pack Leader in Terminator Armour** loses **Pack Leader**.

### Practical effect on requested datasheets

- **Arjac Rockfist:** retains **Deep Strike**, **Leader** and **Anvil of Endurance**, but loses **Champion of the Kingsguard**.
- **Belial:** retains **Deep Strike**, **Leader** and **Grand Master of the Deathwing**, but loses **Strikes of Retribution**.
- **Logan Grimnar:** loses **The Great Wolf** in this detachment.
- **Deathwing Knights:** lose **Inner Circle** in this detachment.
- **Terminator Assault Squad / Terminator Squad:** gain the special Boarding Actions interaction allowing them to **Secure Site** as if **BATTLELINE**.
- **Any datasheet with Teleport Homer:** that ability is removed for Boarding Actions use here.

## 4. Army Rule: Oath of Moment

Units from your army with the **Oath of Moment** ability retain that ability.

### Oath of Moment

At the start of your Command phase, select one enemy unit. Until the start of your next Command phase, each time an **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** model from your army makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, you can re-roll the **Hit roll**.

---

## 5. Detachment Rule: Sacred Oath

### Sacred Oath

Once per battle, at the start of your **Command phase**, when you select a target for **Oath of Moment**, you can use this Detachment rule. If you do, until the start of your next Command phase, each time an **ADEPTUS ASTARTES** model from your army makes an attack that targets your **Oath of Moment** target, you can re-roll the **Wound roll**.

## 6. Enhancements

These are the two Terminator Assault Boarding Actions Enhancements.

### Seal of Indomitability

Once per battle, when the bearer’s unit is selected as the target of one or more attacks, it can use this Enhancement. If it does, until the end of the phase, the bearer has a **3+ invulnerable save**.

### Resolute

- The bearer is eligible to **declare a charge** in a turn in which it **Fell Back**.
- **Desperate Escape tests** taken for the bearer are automatically passed.
- Each time the bearer makes a **Fall Back** move, it can move through enemy models as if they were not there.

## 7. Stratagems

### TOWER OF STRENGTH (1CP)

**Type:** Terminator Assault – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Start or end of any phase.  
**Target:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR** unit from your army.  
**Effect:** Until the end of your next **Command phase**, add **1** to the **Objective Control** characteristic of models in your unit.  
**Restrictions:** Each time you use this Stratagem, you cannot use it again until after your next Command phase.

### FOCUSING SHRINE (1CP)

**Type:** Terminator Assault – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your **Movement phase**, at the start of the **Reinforcements step**.  
**Target:** One **ADEPTUS ASTARTES TERMINATOR** unit from your army that is in **Reserves**.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, when setting up your unit using its **Deep Strike** ability, you can set it up anywhere on the battlefield that is more than **6" horizontally** away from all enemy models, measuring as normal for Deep Strike but ignoring **Walls** and closed **Hatchways**.  
**Restrictions:** Until the end of the turn, your unit is not eligible to **declare a charge**.

### CARVE A PATH (1CP)

**Type:** Terminator Assault – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your **Movement** or **Charge** phase.  
**Target:** One **Terminator Assault Squad** unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and has not declared a charge this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, when making a **Normal**, **Advance** or **Charge** move, your unit can move through a single closed **Hatchway** as if it was open. When doing so, that Hatchway is opened and cannot be closed for the rest of the battle. This can allow that unit to declare a charge against enemy units that are not visible to it, but to do so those enemy units must be within **1"** of that Hatchway and wholly on the opposite side of it from your unit.  
**Restrictions:** You cannot use this Stratagem more than once per turn.

### CLEANSING SWEEP (1CP)

**Type:** Terminator Assault – Battle Tactic Stratagem  
**When:** Your opponent’s **Movement** or **Charge** phase.  
**Target:** One **Terminator Squad** unit from your army that has not fired **Overwatch** this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes an attack while firing **Overwatch**, an unmodified **Hit roll of 5+** is required to score a hit, instead of an unmodified **6**.

## 9. Quick Reference

### Mustering snapshot

- Same **Chapter** only.
- **Up to 2 total, no duplicates** from the listed character pool.
- **Up to 2 of each** listed Terminator unit.

### Main detachment identity

- Normal **Oath of Moment** retained.
- **Sacred Oath** gives a once-per-battle **re-roll Wounds** window against your Oath target.
- Strong defensive/objective tools via **Tower of Strength** and elite breach/teleport tools via **Focusing Shrine** and **Carve a Path**.

### Key Boarding Actions changes

- **Terminator Assault Squad** and **Terminator Squad** gain **Secure Site** as if **BATTLELINE**.
- **Teleport Homer** is removed from all units that have it.
- Specific named/unit abilities are removed exactly as listed under **Rules Adaptations** above.
