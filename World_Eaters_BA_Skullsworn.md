## 1. Detachment Overview

**Faction:** World Eaters  
**Boarding Actions Detachment:** Skullsworn

This Boarding Actions detachment represents a compact elite World Eaters strike force built around Terminators, Eightbound and murderous champions forcing decisive melee fights inside the close confines of voidships and space hulks.

### Included Unit Roster

| Slot | Unit | Boarding Actions Limit |
|---|---|---:|
| Character | **Khârn The Betrayer** | Shared pool: up to 2 total with Master of Executions |
| Character | **Master of Executions** | Shared pool: up to 2 total with Khârn The Betrayer |
| Elite | **Eightbound** | Up to 2 units |
| Elite | **Exalted Eightbound** | Up to 2 units |
| Elite | **Chaos Terminators** | Up to 2 units |

## 2. Mustering a Boarding Patrol

When mustering a **Skullsworn** Boarding Patrol, you can include:

- Up to **two of the following units**:
  - **Khârn The Betrayer**
  - **World Eaters Master of Executions**
- Up to **two of each** of the following units:
  - **Eightbound** (**3 models**)
  - **Exalted Eightbound** (**3 models**)
  - **World Eaters Terminator Squad** (**5 or 10 models**)

### Boarding Actions size notes

- **Eightbound:** Boarding Actions uses the **3-model** unit.
- **Exalted Eightbound:** Boarding Actions uses the **3-model** unit.
- **Chaos Terminators / World Eaters Terminator Squad:** the Skullsworn mustering entry allows **5- or 10-model** units.

## 3. Rules Adaptations

The Skullsworn Boarding Actions entry lists the following adaptations:

- **World Eaters Terminator Squad** units can perform the **Secure Site** Tactical Manoeuvre as if they had the **BATTLELINE** keyword.
- **Exalted Eightbound** lose **Overwhelming Wrath**.
- **Khârn The Betrayer** loses **The Betrayer**.

### Practical effect on requested datasheets

- **Khârn The Betrayer:** retains **Leader**, **Blessings of Khorne**, **Legendary Killer** and **Berzerker Frenzy**, but **loses The Betrayer** in Boarding Actions.
- **Master of Executions:** no Skullsworn datasheet loss listed.
- **Eightbound:** no Skullsworn datasheet loss listed.
- **Exalted Eightbound:** retain **Deep Strike** and **Rend and Tear**, but **lose Overwhelming Wrath**.
- **Chaos Terminators:** no datasheet loss listed; they gain the special Boarding Actions interaction allowing them to **Secure Site** as if **BATTLELINE**.

## 4. Army Rule: Slaughterers' Glory

Units from your army with the **Blessings of Khorne** ability retain that ability and gain **Slaughterers' Glory**.

### Slaughterers' Glory

At the start of the **battle round**, when making a **Blessings of Khorne** roll:

- roll **four D6** instead of **eight D6**;
- you can activate only **one Blessing of Khorne** per battle round, instead of up to **two**.

---

## 5. Blessings of Khorne Reference

The Skullsworn detachment keeps the normal **Blessings of Khorne** rule, but modifies the number of dice rolled and the number of Blessings that can be activated via **Slaughterers' Glory**.

### Blessings of Khorne procedure

If your Army Faction is **WORLD EATERS**, at the start of the battle round you make a Blessings of Khorne roll.

- In normal play you roll **eight D6** and can activate up to **two** Blessings.
- In **Skullsworn**, because of **Slaughterers' Glory**, you instead roll **four D6** and can activate only **one** Blessing.
- Once activated, a Blessing applies to all units from your army with the **Blessings of Khorne** ability until the end of the battle round.

### Blessings

| Blessing | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Unbridled Bloodlust** | You can re-roll **Charge** rolls made for this unit. |
| **Rage-fuelled Invigoration** | Each time a model in this unit makes a **Pile-in** or **Consolidation** move, it can move up to **6"** instead of up to **3"**. |
| **Total Carnage** | Each time a model in this unit is destroyed by a **melee attack**, if it has not fought this phase, roll one D6: on a **4+**, do not remove it from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play. |
| **Martial Excellence** | Melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have **[SUSTAINED HITS 1]**. |
| **Warp Blades** | Melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have **[LETHAL HITS]**. |
| **Decapitating Strikes** | Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack that targets an **INFANTRY** unit, that attack has **[DEVASTATING WOUNDS]**. |

## 6. Detachment Rule: Reap the Tally

### Reap the Tally

Each time a **WORLD EATERS** model from your army (**excluding Khârn The Betrayer**) is destroyed by a **melee attack** and it has not fought this phase, roll one D6:

- on a **5+**, do not remove it from play;
- the destroyed model can **fight** after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks;
- it is then removed from play.

This stacks thematically with the faction's normal blood-frenzied playstyle, but it is a distinct Skullsworn detachment rule and it applies only to qualifying destroyed models.

## 7. Enhancements

These are the two Skullsworn Boarding Actions Enhancements.

### Frenzied

You can **re-roll Advance and Charge rolls** made for the bearer, and the bearer is eligible to **declare a charge** in a turn in which it **Advanced**.

### Carmine Corona

When the bearer destroys an enemy unit for the first time, until the end of the battle, the bearer has an **Objective Control** characteristic of **5**.

## 8. Stratagems

The Skullsworn Boarding Actions entry lists four detachment Stratagems.

### BLOOD RITE (1CP)

**Type:** Skullsworn – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** **Fight phase**, just after a **WORLD EATERS TERMINATOR**, **EIGHTBOUND** or **EXALTED EIGHTBOUND** unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.  
**Target:** That unit.  
**Effect:** Select one **objective marker** that your unit or the destroyed unit was within range of at the start of the phase. That objective marker is **Secured** by your army.

### FURIOUS MOMENTUM (1CP)

**Type:** Skullsworn – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** **Fight phase**.  
**Target:** One **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time a model in your unit makes a **Pile-in** or **Consolidation** move, it can move up to **6"** instead of up to **3"**.

### RUINOUS RAMPAGE (1CP)

**Type:** Skullsworn – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your **Movement** or **Charge phase**.  
**Target:** One **WORLD EATERS TERMINATOR**, **EIGHTBOUND** or **EXALTED EIGHTBOUND** unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and has not declared a charge this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, when making a **Normal**, **Advance** or **Charge** move, your unit can move through a single closed **Hatchway** as if it was open. When doing so, that Hatchway is opened and cannot be closed for the rest of the battle. This can allow that unit to declare a charge against enemy units that are not visible to it, but to do so those enemy units must be within **1"** of that Hatchway and wholly on the opposite side of it from your unit.  
**Restrictions:** You can only use this Stratagem **once per turn**.

### SHOCKING ONSLAUGHT (1CP)

**Type:** Skullsworn – Battle Tactic Stratagem  
**When:** Your **Movement** or **Charge phase**, just after an enemy unit is selected to fire **Overwatch**.  
**Target:** One **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time an enemy unit is selected to fire **Overwatch**, it must take a **Leadership test**, subtracting **1** from the result; if failed, that enemy unit cannot fire **Overwatch** at your unit this phase.

## 10. Quick Reference

### Skullsworn Core Rules Summary

- **Army Rule:** **Slaughterers' Glory** modifies **Blessings of Khorne** to **4D6** and only **1** active Blessing each battle round.
- **Detachment Rule:** **Reap the Tally** lets qualifying destroyed models fight on a **5+** after being destroyed by melee.
- **Secure Site interaction:** **Chaos Terminators** can perform **Secure Site** as if they were **BATTLELINE**.
- **Key datasheet losses:** **Khârn** loses **The Betrayer**; **Exalted Eightbound** lose **Overwhelming Wrath**.

### Mustering Snapshot

- Up to **two total** from **Khârn The Betrayer** and **Master of Executions**.
- Up to **two each** of **Eightbound**, **Exalted Eightbound**, and **Chaos Terminators**.
- Eightbound entries use **3-model** units; Terminators use **5- or 10-model** units per the Skullsworn page.

### Stratagem Snapshot

- **Blood Rite:** Secure an objective after elite killers destroy an enemy unit.
- **Furious Momentum:** Extend Pile-in and Consolidation to **6"**.
- **Ruinous Rampage:** Move through a closed Hatchway and potentially charge through it.
- **Shocking Onslaught:** Force Leadership tests to block Overwatch.
