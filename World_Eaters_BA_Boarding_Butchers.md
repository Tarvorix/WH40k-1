## 1. Detachment Overview

**Faction:** World Eaters  
**Boarding Actions Detachment:** Boarding Butchers

This Boarding Actions detachment represents a brutal close-quarters World Eaters assault force built around Berzerkers, Jakhals and elite killers smashing through cramped voidship corridors. Its rules emphasize shock assault, morale collapse and relentless melee pressure.

### Included Unit Roster

| Slot | Unit | Boarding Actions Limit |
|---|---|---:|
| Character | **Khârn The Betrayer** | Up to 2 |
| Character | **Master of Executions** | Up to 2 |
| Mainline | **Khorne Berzerkers** | Up to 3 units |
| Mainline | **Jakhals** | Up to 3 units* |
| Elite | **Eightbound** | Up to 1 unit |
| Elite | **Exalted Eightbound** | Up to 1 unit |
| Support | **Chaos Spawn** | Up to 1 unit |
| Elite | **Chaos Terminators** | Up to 1 unit |

\* The combined total number of **Jakhals** units cannot be more than the number of **Khorne Berzerkers** units.

## 2. Mustering a Boarding Patrol

When mustering a **Boarding Butchers** Boarding Patrol, you can include:

- Up to **2** of the following units:
  - **Khârn The Betrayer**
  - **Master of Executions**
- Up to **3** of each of the following units:
  - **Khorne Berzerkers** (**5 or 10 models** in Boarding Actions)
  - **Jakhals** (**10 models**)
- The combined total number of **Jakhals** units cannot be more than the number of **Khorne Berzerkers** units.
- Up to **1** of the following units:
  - **Eightbound** (**3 models**)
  - **Exalted Eightbound** (**3 models**)
- Up to **1** of each of the following units:
  - **Chaos Spawn** (**2 models**)
  - **Chaos Terminators** (**5 models**)

### Boarding Actions size notes

Several of these Boarding Actions unit sizes differ from the full faction datasheet sizes:

- **Khorne Berzerkers:** Boarding Actions allows **5 or 10**; the full datasheet is **10 or 20**.
- **Jakhals:** Boarding Actions allows **10**; the full datasheet is **10 or 20**.
- **Eightbound / Exalted Eightbound:** Boarding Actions allows the **3-model** unit only.
- **Chaos Terminators:** Boarding Actions allows the **5-model** unit only.

## 5. Army Rule: Slaughterers' Glory

Units from your army with the **Blessings of Khorne** ability retain that ability and gain **Slaughterers' Glory**.

### Slaughterers' Glory

At the start of the **battle round**, when making a **Blessings of Khorne** roll:

- roll **four D6** instead of **eight D6**;
- you can activate only **one Blessing of Khorne** per battle round, instead of up to **two**.

This is the key World Eaters Boarding Actions modification shared by Boarding Butchers and Skullsworn.

---

## 6. Blessings of Khorne Reference

The Boarding Butchers detachment keeps the normal **Blessings of Khorne** rule, but modifies the number of dice rolled and the number of Blessings that can be activated via **Slaughterers' Glory**.

### Blessings of Khorne procedure

If your Army Faction is **WORLD EATERS**, at the start of the battle round you make a Blessings of Khorne roll.

- In normal play you roll **eight D6** and can activate up to **two** Blessings.
- In **Boarding Butchers**, because of **Slaughterers' Glory**, you instead roll **four D6** and can activate only **one** Blessing.
- Once activated, a Blessing applies to all units from your army with the **Blessings of Khorne** ability until the end of the battle round.

### Blessings

| Blessing | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Unbridled Bloodlust** | You can re-roll **Charge** rolls made for this unit. |
| **Rage-fuelled Invigoration** | Each time a model in this unit makes a **Pile-in** or **Consolidation** move, it can move up to **6\"** instead of up to **3\"**. |
| **Total Carnage** | Each time a model in this unit is destroyed by a **melee attack**, if it has not fought this phase, roll one D6: on a **4+**, do not remove it from play; it can fight after the attacking unit has finished making its attacks, and is then removed from play. |
| **Martial Excellence** | Melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have **[SUSTAINED HITS 1]**. |
| **Warp Blades** | Melee weapons equipped by models in this unit have **[LETHAL HITS]**. |
| **Decapitating Strikes** | Each time a model in this unit makes a melee attack that targets an **INFANTRY** unit, that attack has **[DEVASTATING WOUNDS]**. |

## 7. Detachment Rule: Focused Ferocity

### Focused Ferocity

Each time a **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army declares a charge, each target of that charge must take a **Battle-shock test**.

This rule is exceptionally strong in Boarding Actions because the battlefield is narrow, fallback paths are limited and multiple later effects in the detachment can punish Battle-shocked or retreating enemy units.

## 8. Enhancements

These are the two Boarding Butchers Boarding Actions Enhancements.

### Chosen of Khorne

Many are the skulls this warrior has taken and offered up in the Blood God's name, which has its rewards.

**Effect:** Once the bearer has destroyed one or more enemy units, until the end of the battle, each time you make a **Blessings of Khorne** roll, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can **re-roll all of the dice**. If you do, you must re-roll **every die**.

### Battle Lust

Foes attempting to gun this warrior down only stoke his berserk rage still higher.

**Effect:** Each time an enemy unit is selected to shoot, after that unit has shot, if the bearer was the target of one or more of those attacks, it can make a **Battle Lust move**. Roll one D6; the bearer can be moved a distance in inches up to the result, but must end that move **as close as possible** to the attacking unit. When doing so, the bearer can be moved within **Engagement Range** of that enemy unit, but only that enemy unit.

## 9. Stratagems

The Boarding Butchers Boarding Actions entry lists four detachment Stratagems.

### TERRIFYING SCREAMS (1CP)

**Type:** Boarding Butchers – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** **Fight phase**, just after a **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army destroys an enemy unit.  
**Target:** That **WORLD EATERS** unit.  
**Effect:** Until the end of your opponent's next **Command phase**, subtract **1** from **Battle-shock** and **Leadership** tests taken for all enemy units.

### UNSTOPPABLE RAGE (1CP)

**Type:** Boarding Butchers – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your **Movement phase**, just after a **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army (**excluding JAKHAL units**) opens a **Hatchway**.  
**Target:** That unit.  
**Effect:** For each enemy unit within **3\"** of that Hatchway and wholly on the opposite side of it from your unit, until the start of your next Movement phase, that enemy unit is **stunned**. While a unit is stunned, each time a model in it makes an attack, subtract **1** from the **Hit roll**.

### SAVAGE RESILIENCE (1CP)

**Type:** Boarding Butchers – Battle Tactic Stratagem  
**When:** Your opponent's **Shooting phase**, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.  
**Target:** One **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of that attacking unit's attacks.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time an attack is allocated to a model in your unit, subtract **1** from the **Damage** characteristic of that attack.

### COWARDS' BANE (1CP)

**Type:** Boarding Butchers – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your opponent's **Movement phase**, just after an enemy unit is selected to **Fall Back**.  
**Target:** One **WORLD EATERS** unit from your army (**excluding JAKHAL units**) within **Engagement Range** of that enemy unit.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time that enemy unit Falls Back, models in it must take **Desperate Escape tests as if their unit was Battle-shocked**. If that enemy unit is also Battle-shocked by other means, subtract **1** from each of those Desperate Escape tests.
