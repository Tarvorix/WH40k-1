## 1. Army Overview

The Frenzied Reavers are a World Eaters Combat Patrol led by the terrifying Daemon Prince Vorrakh. This force embodies the brutal melee philosophy of Khorne - overwhelm enemies with furious close combat assaults while the Blessings of the Blood God empower your warriors to fight beyond death itself.

### Army Characteristics
- **Melee Dominance**: Nearly all damage output comes from close combat
- **High Model Count**: 22 models total provides board presence
- **Fight on Death**: Multiple abilities allow destroyed models to fight before removal
- **Dice Manipulation**: Blessings of Khorne provides unique buff system each round
- **Character Hunting**: Master of Executions specializes in killing enemy leaders
- **Glass Cannon Elements**: Jakhals are fragile but numerous

### Model Count
- **Total Models**: 22
  - 1 Vorrakh (Daemon Prince)
  - 1 Master of Executions
  - 10 Khorne Berzerkers
  - 10 Jakhals

### Playstyle Summary
Rush forward aggressively, use Blessings of Khorne to enhance your combat abilities, and drown opponents in melee attacks. Accept casualties - your warriors can often fight back even as they die.

### Unit Roles Explained

**Vorrakh (Daemon Prince)**
- Your most durable model with T10, 10 wounds, 2+ save, 4+ invuln

- Dedicated character killer with Precision and Devastating Wounds
- Re-rolls Hit AND Wound against CHARACTER units

**Khorne Berzerkers**
- The backbone of your force - 10 elite melee infantry

- Can trigger enemy Battle-shock tests via Horrifying Butchery

**Jakhals**
- Expendable cultist-tier troops (T4, 1W, 6+ save)
- Use for screening, objective grabbing, and dying for Khorne
- Bloodlust Stratagem lets them move D6" after being shot
- Can sneak onto objectives while enemies focus on bigger threats

## 3. Faction Ability: Blessings of Khorne

### Overview
All WORLD EATERS units in this Combat Patrol have the **Blessings of Khorne** faction ability. This unique mechanic lets you roll dice at the start of each battle round and spend them to activate powerful buffs.

### How It Works

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  BLESSINGS OF KHORNE                            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │
│  TIMING: Start of each battle round                             │
│                                                                 │
│  PROCEDURE:                                                     │
│  1. Roll 5D6 (the "Blessings of Khorne roll")                   │
│  2. Look at the results                                         │
│  3. Use dice to activate up to TWO Blessings                    │
│  4. Each Blessing can only be activated once per round          │
│  5. Unused dice are discarded                                   │
│  6. Active Blessings apply to ALL units with this ability       │
│     until the end of the battle round                           │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Blessing Selection Strategy

**Standard Priority (Most Rounds):**
1. **Martial Excellence** (if available) - raw damage increase
2. **Total Carnage** - fight on death insurance
3. **Rage-fuelled Invigoration** - mobility if needed

**When Expecting Heavy Casualties:**
1. **Total Carnage** - maximize fight-back potential
2. **Martial Excellence** - make each attack count

**When You Need Board Control:**
1. **Rage-fuelled Invigoration** - 6" pile-in/consolidate
2. **Martial Excellence** - kill efficiency

### Dice Allocation Examples

```
Example 1: Roll = 2, 3, 4, 4, 6
→ Use 4+4 for Martial Excellence (Double 4+)
→ Use 2+3 for Rage-fuelled Invigoration (Double 2+)
→ Discard the 6
→ Both Blessings active!

Example 2: Roll = 1, 1, 2, 3, 5
→ Use 2+3 for Total Carnage (Double 2+)
→ Use 1+5 for Rage-fuelled Invigoration (Double 2+ uses the 5)
   Wait - 1 isn't 2+! Need to use 3+5 instead
→ Use 2+5 for Total Carnage, 3+3... no second 3
→ Actually: Use 2+3 for one blessing, 5 alone can't activate anything
→ Only one Blessing this round (Total Carnage or Rage-fuelled)

Example 3: Roll = 3, 3, 3, 5, 6
→ Use 3+3+3 for Martial Excellence (Triple!)
→ Use 5+6 for Total Carnage (Double 2+)
→ Both Blessings active!
```

## 9. Tactical Guide

### Army Strengths

1. **Overwhelming Melee**
   - 22 models producing 80+ melee attacks per turn
   - Vorrakh alone has 16 attacks
   - Berzerkers have 38 attacks combined
   - Martial Excellence adds Sustained Hits 1 to everything

2. **Fight on Death**
   - Total Carnage Blessing: 50% to fight when killed
   - Berserk Resilience Stratagem: 50-83% for CHARACTERs
   - Forces opponents to reconsider attacking you

3. **Board Presence**
   - 22 models is high for Combat Patrol
   - OC spread: 3 (Vorrakh) + 1 (MoE) + 20 (Berzerkers) + 10 (Jakhals) = 34 total OC
   - Can contest multiple objectives

4. **Character Assassination**
   - Master of Executions re-rolls everything vs CHARACTERs
   - Devastating Wounds + Precision = dead enemy leaders
   - Gains CP when killing enemy CHARACTERs

5. **Unique Buff System**
   - Blessings of Khorne gives flexible buffs each round
   - High activation rates on critical abilities
   - Adapts to tactical needs

### Army Weaknesses

1. **Limited Shooting**
   - Only ranged damage: Infernal cannon (3-6 shots), pistols
   - Must close to melee to deal significant damage
   - Vulnerable to kiting

2. **Jakhals Are Fragile**
   - 10 models at T4, 1W, 6+ save
   - Will die to almost any dedicated firepower
   - Poor Leadership (7+) means frequent Battle-shock

3. **No Invulnerable Saves (except Vorrakh)**
   - Berzerkers and MoE rely on 3+ armor
   - AP-2 or better shreds them
   - Jakhals have nothing but T4

4. **Character Vulnerability**
   - Master of Executions is fragile
   - Losing Vorrakh cripples your damage and durability
   - No Bodyguard mechanics

5. **Blessing Variance**
   - Sometimes you roll poorly and get weak Blessings
   - Martial Excellence has ~55% activation chance
   - Can't rely on specific Blessings

### General Strategy

#### Deployment
1. **Screen with Jakhals** - put them in front to absorb shooting
2. **Berzerkers behind** - ready to charge through gaps
3. **Vorrakh central** - can support either flank
4. **Master of Executions hidden** - keep him safe until he can strike

#### Turn 1
- **Roll Blessings** - aim for Martial Excellence and/or Total Carnage
- **Advance everything** - close the distance aggressively
- Jakhals absorb Overwatch and early shooting
- Position for Turn 2 charges

#### Turn 2+
- **Charge with everything** - multi-charges where possible
- Use **Horrifying Butchery** to Battle-shock priority targets
- **Vorrakh on objectives** with Fearsome Presence (OC 5)
- Master of Executions **hunts enemy characters**

#### Combat
- **Choose Blessings based on phase**:
  - Martial Excellence for killing
  - Total Carnage if expecting heavy losses
  - Rage-fuelled Invigoration for mobility
- Use **Counter-Offensive** to interrupt enemy attacks
- **Berserk Resilience** on CHARACTERs being attacked

#### Objective Play
- Jakhals **grab distant objectives** - expendable
- Berzerkers **clear and hold** central objectives
- Vorrakh **dominates** any objective he's on (OC 5)
- Don't spread too thin - concentrate force

### Matchup Considerations

#### vs. Elite Armies (Custodes, Knights, Marines)
- **Focus fire** one unit at a time
- Use **Khornate eviscerators** (S8 D2) on tough targets
- **Master of Executions** hunts their characters
- **Total Carnage** - if they kill Berzerkers, Berzerkers fight back
- **Champions of Khorne** secondary - control objectives with CHARACTERs

#### vs. Horde Armies (Orks, Tyranids, Guard)
- **Martial Excellence** for Sustained Hits 1
- Vorrakh's **16 attacks** mow through infantry
- **Berzerkers** are designed to kill hordes
- Watch out for **weight of attacks** overwhelming your saves
- **Skull Takers** secondary - many units to destroy

#### vs. Shooting Armies (Tau, Guard, Eldar)
- **Advance aggressively** - every turn you're not in melee is bad
- Use **Jakhals to screen** and absorb fire
- **Bloodlust** when Jakhals are shot to move closer
- **Bane of the Craven** punishes Fall Back attempts
- Terrain is critical - use cover

#### vs. Psyker Armies (Thousand Sons, Grey Knights)
- Khorne hates psykers but has no defense against them
- **Rush them down** before they can cast too much
- Focus the psykers with Master of Executions
- Accept that mortal wounds will happen
