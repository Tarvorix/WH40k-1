# Chaos Space Marines Boarding Actions — Underdeck Uprising

A comprehensive Boarding Actions reference for the **Chaos Space Marines – Underdeck Uprising** detachment and the requested eligible datasheets.

---

## 1. Detachment Overview

**Detachment:** Underdeck Uprising  
**Faction:** Chaos Space Marines  
**Boarding Actions Source:** Wahapedia Boarding Actions section for Chaos Space Marines

> It takes only the slightest seed of corruption to spread sedition and heresy through the downtrodden and desperate crew-serfs packed into many voidships’ bilges and gun decks. Whipped into a traitorous frenzy by demagogues promises of power, mutants and madmen rise up en masse to seize the ship.

---

## 2. Mustering a Boarding Patrol

When mustering an **Underdeck Uprising** Boarding Patrol, you can include:

- **Up to one** of the following unit:
  - **Dark Apostle**

- **Up to two** of the following units (**duplicates are not allowed**):
  - **Cultist Firebrand**
  - **Dark Commune**

- **Up to three of each** of the following units:
  - **Accursed Cultists** (**8 models**)
  - **Cultist Mob** (**10 models**)
  - **Fellgor Beastmen** (**10 models**)

- **Up to two** of the following unit:
  - **Chaos Spawn** (**2 models**)

### Boarding Actions size notes

These are the Boarding Actions legal sizes shown on the detachment entry:

- **Accursed Cultists:** **8 models**
- **Cultist Mob:** **10 models**
- **Fellgor Beastmen:** **10 models**
- **Chaos Spawn:** **2 models**

---

## 3. Forming Boarding Squads

During the **Form Boarding Squads** step:

- **Cultist Mob** units do **not** split into separate units.
- **Fellgor Beastmen** units do **not** split into separate units.

---

## 4. Rules Adaptations

The Underdeck Uprising Boarding Actions entry lists the following datasheet changes:

- **Accursed Cultists** lose **Howling Horde**.
- **Cultist Firebrand** loses **Fiery Faith**.
- **Cultist Mob** loses **For the Dark Gods**.
- **Dark Apostle** loses **Malign Sacrifice**.

### Practical effect on requested datasheets

- **Dark Apostle:** keeps **Dark Zealotry** and **Demagogue**, but loses **Malign Sacrifice** in Boarding Actions.
- **Cultist Firebrand:** keeps **Leader** and **Cursed Flames**, but loses **Fiery Faith** in Boarding Actions.
- **Dark Commune:** no Boarding Actions-specific datasheet loss is listed.
- **Accursed Cultists:** keep **Feel No Pain 6+**, **Scouts 6\"** and **Accursed Horde**, but lose **Howling Horde** in Boarding Actions.
- **Cultist Mob:** keeps **Dark Pacts** and its normal datasheet profile, but loses **For the Dark Gods** in Boarding Actions.
- **Fellgor Beastmen:** no Boarding Actions-specific datasheet loss is listed.
- **Chaos Spawn:** no Underdeck Uprising datasheet loss is listed.

## 5. Army Rule

## The Forsaken

Only the strong may retain the favour of the Dark Gods. The weak are cast aside, condemned to an existence of paltry mortality.

Units from your army with the **Dark Pacts** ability retain that ability and gain this one:

- Each time this unit takes a Leadership test for the **Dark Pacts** ability, if that test is failed, this unit is **Forsaken** and, until the end of the battle, this unit loses the **Dark Pacts** ability.

## 6. Detachment Rule

## Fanatical Onslaught

Knowing their only options are victory or death, the heretic hordes pack corridors with their bodies as they surge forward into the teeth of the enemy guns like a zealous flood tide.

- In your opponent’s **Shooting phase**, each time an enemy unit has shot, if one or more models from one or more **HERETIC ASTARTES** units from your army were destroyed as a result of those attacks, each such unit can make a **Rush move**.
- To do so, that unit can be moved up to **3\"**, but must end that move **as close as possible** to the attacking unit.
- When doing so, those models can be moved within **Engagement Range** of enemy units.
- A unit cannot make a Rush move while it is **Battle-shocked**, and can only make **one Rush move per turn**.

## 7. Enhancements

### Rabble Rouser

This warrior’s impassioned heretic oratory whips their dreg followers into a violent frenzy.

- Once per turn, when a friendly **Cultist Mob** or **Fellgor Beastmen** unit within **6\"** of the bearer is selected to shoot or fight, the bearer can use this Enhancement.
- Until the end of the phase, add **1** to the **Attacks** characteristic of weapons equipped by models in that friendly unit.

### Vox Malevolus

This grotesque biomechanoid helm amplifies its wearer’s words through a daemonic grille-maw that fills their followers with supernatural aggression and urgency.

- In the first **battle round**, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can **re-roll Advance rolls** made for **HERETIC ASTARTES** units from your army.

## 8. Stratagems

### INFERNAL ALTARS — 1CP
**Underdeck Uprising – Battle Tactic Stratagem**

Whether heretical fanes unveiled amidst the rebellion or heaped corpses and votive offerings, these ritual foci lend the faithful supernatural protection.

**When:** Your opponent’s **Shooting phase** or the **Fight phase**, just after an enemy unit has selected its targets.  
**Target:** One **DAMNED** unit from your army that was selected as the target of one or more of the attacking unit’s attacks.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, each time an attack targets your unit, if your unit is within range of an **objective marker**, models in your unit have **Feel No Pain 5+** against that attack. If your unit has the **Cultist Mob** keyword, models in it have **Feel No Pain 4+** against that attack instead.

### FLEETING MIGHT — 1CP
**Underdeck Uprising – Battle Tactic Stratagem**

For one tantalising instant, the dark prayers of these fanatics are answered by some infernal warp patron, placing the power to slay champions in their hands.

**When:** Your **Shooting phase** or the **Fight phase**, just after a **DAMNED** unit from your army makes a **Dark Pact**.  
**Target:** That **DAMNED** unit.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, weapons equipped by models in your unit have **[DEVASTATING WOUNDS]**.

### CRUDE SABOTAGE — 1CP
**Underdeck Uprising – Strategic Ploy Stratagem**

Using charges rigged from scavenged junk and pollutant runoff, the fanatical masses blast open the hatchways that bar their paths to ascension.

**When:** Your **Movement phase**.  
**Target:** One **Cultist Mob** or **DAMNED CHARACTER** unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase and is not within **Engagement Range** of one or more enemy units.  
**Effect:** Select one closed **Hatchway** within **1\"** of your unit. Your unit can attempt to operate that Hatchway.  
**Restrictions:** Until the end of the turn, your unit cannot attempt to **operate a Hatchway** again.

### STORM THE BRIDGE! — 1CP
**Underdeck Uprising – Strategic Ploy Stratagem**

Seizing their opportunity to strike a fatal blow against those who have oppressed them for so long — and please the Dark Gods in the process — these heretics surge forwards with zealous ferocity.

**When:** Your **Charge phase**.  
**Target:** One **DAMNED** or **Chaos Spawn** unit from your army.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, your unit is eligible to **declare a charge** in a turn in which it **Advanced**.

### Abilities
**Core:** Leader  
**Faction:** Dark Pacts

- **Dark Zealotry:** While this unit is leading a unit and contains a **Dark Apostle** model, each time a model in that unit makes a melee attack, add **1** to the **Wound roll**.
- **Demagogue:** Once per battle, at the start of **any phase**, you can select one friendly **HERETIC ASTARTES** unit that is **Battle-shocked** and within **12\"** of this unit’s Dark Apostle model. That unit is no longer Battle-shocked.
- **Malign Sacrifice:** At the start of the **Fight phase**, if this unit contains one or more Dark Disciple models, you can select one of those models and one enemy unit within Engagement Range of this unit, then roll one D6: on a **2-5**, that enemy unit suffers **1 mortal wound**; on a **6**, that enemy unit suffers **D3 mortal wounds**. That Dark Disciple model is then destroyed.

- **Accursed Cultists**
- **Chosen**
- **Cultist Mob**
- **Legionaries**
- **Nemesis Claw**
- **Red Corsairs Raiders**

### Boarding Actions interaction
- In **Underdeck Uprising**, this unit **loses Malign Sacrifice**.
