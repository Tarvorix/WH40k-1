## 1. Detachment Overview

**Faction:** Astra Militarum  
**Boarding Actions Detachment:** Tempestus Boarding Regiment

This Boarding Actions detachment represents a void-assault force built around Militarum Tempestus storm troops, supported by a small cadre of attached officers, psykers and breacher-grade abhumans. Its rules focus on compact-room assault, hatchway control and short-range fire discipline.

### Included Unit Roster

| Slot | Unit | Boarding Actions Limit |
|---|---|---:|
| Character | **Militarum Tempestus Command Squad** | Up to 1 |
| Character | **Commissar** | Up to 1 |
| Character | **Primaris Psyker** | Up to 1 |
| Mainline | **Tempestus Scions** | Up to 3 units |
| Support | **Bullgryn Squad** | Up to 1 unit |

## 2. Mustering a Boarding Patrol

When mustering a **Tempestus Boarding Regiment** Boarding Patrol, you can include:

- Up to **1 Militarum Tempestus Command Squad**
- Up to **1 Commissar**
- Up to **1 Primaris Psyker**
- Up to **3 Tempestus Scions units** (each unit can be **5 or 10 models**)
- Up to **1 Bullgryn Squad** (**3 models**)

### Explicitly Excluded on the Wahapedia Boarding Actions entry

The Tempestus Boarding Regiment entry also shows the following as struck out / not selectable here:

- Ogryn Bodyguard
- Regimental Enginseer
- Regimental Preacher

For this requested Tempestus Boarding Regiment roster, none of the listed selectable units has a currently selectable Loyal Protector model available inside this detachment list, but the detachment rule still states this requirement generically.

## 4. Rules Adaptations

The Tempestus Boarding Regiment Boarding Actions page lists the following adaptations:

- **Tempestus Scions** units can perform the **Secure Site** Tactical Manoeuvre **as if they had the BATTLELINE keyword**.
- **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** units lose the **Deep Strike** ability in Boarding Actions play.

### Practical effect on requested datasheets

This means the following requested units are affected as follows:

- **Militarum Tempestus Command Squad**: printed with **Deep Strike**, but loses it in this Boarding Actions detachment.
- **Tempestus Scions**: printed with **Deep Strike**, but lose it in this Boarding Actions detachment; they also gain the ability to perform **Secure Site** as though they were BATTLELINE.
- **Commissar**, **Primaris Psyker**, and **Bullgryn Squad**: no special Tempestus-specific rules adaptation beyond normal Boarding Actions applicability.

### Master Vox adaptation

The Astra Militarum Boarding Actions rules also adapt **Master Vox**:

- **Master Vox** only allows the unit’s OFFICER to issue Orders to units up to **12"** away instead of **24"**.
- When doing so, **Walls** and **closed Hatchways** are ignored.

## 5. Army Rule: Direct Orders

Units from your army with the **Voice of Command** ability retain that ability and gain **Direct Orders**.

### Direct Orders

Each time an **OFFICER** model in this unit issues an Order, that Order can be issued to any one friendly **ASTRA MILITARUM** unit within range, **regardless of any keywords that unit is normally required to have**.

This is the key Boarding Actions Astra Militarum army rule for both the Embarked Regiment and Tempestus Boarding Regiment boarding detachments.

## 6. Orders Reference

### Voice of Command - core order procedure

If your Army Faction is **ASTRA MILITARUM**, **OFFICER** models with **Voice of Command** can issue Orders.

- Each OFFICER datasheet specifies how many Orders it can issue each battle round and which units are eligible.
- Each time an OFFICER issues an Order, select one of the Orders below, then select one eligible friendly unit within **6"** to issue it to.
- OFFICER models can issue Orders in your **Command phase** and at the end of a phase in which they **disembarked from a Transport** or were **set up on the battlefield**.
- Unless otherwise stated, a unit can only be affected by **one Order at a time**.
- Orders cannot be issued to **Battle-shocked** units, and if an affected unit becomes Battle-shocked, that Order ceases to apply.
- Only **ASTRA MILITARUM** models gain the benefit of an Order issued to their unit.

### Orders

| Order | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Move! Move! Move!** | Add **3"** to the **Move** characteristic of models in this unit. |
| **Fix Bayonets!** | Improve the **Weapon Skill** characteristic of melee weapons equipped by models in this unit by **1**. |
| **Take Aim!** | Improve the **Ballistic Skill** characteristic of ranged weapons equipped by models in this unit by **1**. |
| **First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire!** | Improve the **Attacks** characteristic of **Rapid Fire** weapons equipped by models in this unit by **1**. |
| **Take Cover!** | Improve the **Save** characteristic of models in this unit by **1** (to a maximum of **3+**). |
| **Duty and Honour!** | Improve the **Leadership** and **Objective Control** characteristics of models in this unit by **1**. |

### Orders actually available from requested characters

| Unit | Orders Issued | Eligible Target Type | Restrictions |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Militarum Tempestus Command Squad** | 1 | REGIMENT unit | None beyond normal eligibility |
| **Commissar** | 1 | REGIMENT unit | Can only issue **Duty and Honour!** and **Fix Bayonets!** |
| **Primaris Psyker** | 0 | — | No Voice of Command |

Because of **Direct Orders**, those eligible Orders can be issued to any one friendly **ASTRA MILITARUM** unit within range, regardless of the usual keyword limitations.

## 7. Detachment Rule: Purge-Sweep Protocols

### Purge-Sweep Protocols

Each time a **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** unit from your army is selected to shoot, if:

- one or more models in that unit are within **1" of an open Hatchway**, and
- every model in that unit is on the **same side** of that Hatchway,

select one enemy unit on the **opposite side** of that Hatchway from that unit. Until the end of the phase, ranged weapons equipped by models in that unit have **[LETHAL HITS]** while targeting that enemy unit.

### Units in this requested roster that can benefit

- **Militarum Tempestus Command Squad**
- **Tempestus Scions**

The **Commissar**, **Primaris Psyker**, and **Bullgryn Squad** do not have the **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** keyword and therefore do not benefit from this detachment rule.

## 8. Enhancements

These are the two Boarding Actions Enhancements listed for the Tempestus Boarding Regiment.

### Covert Breach

Moving with stealth and urgency, these Scions have already bypassed the enemy’s outer defences before their presence is even detected.

**Effect:** At the start of the first battle round, the bearer’s unit can make a **Normal move of up to 6"**.

### Elimination Force

Tasked with the destruction of a specific enemy asset, the Tempestus Scions focus their most lethal attentions against it as per their explicit orders.

**Effect:** At the start of the first battle round, select one enemy unit. Each time a model in the bearer’s unit makes an attack that targets that enemy unit, if the **Strength** characteristic of that attack is **less** than the **Toughness** characteristic of that enemy unit, add **1** to the **Wound roll**.

## 9. Stratagems

The Tempestus Boarding Regiment Boarding Actions entry lists four detachment Stratagems.

### BRUTAL TRAINING (1CP)

**Type:** Tempestus Boarding Regiment – Battle Tactic Stratagem  
**When:** Fight phase  
**Target:** One **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** unit from your army that has not been selected to fight this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, add **1** to the **Attacks** and **Strength** characteristics of melee weapons equipped by models in your unit.

### COORDINATED STRIKE (1CP)

**Type:** Tempestus Boarding Regiment – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Start of your Command phase  
**Target:** One **OFFICER** model from your army.  
**Effect:** Select one Order your model can issue. **Every ASTRA MILITARUM unit** from your army is issued that Order, regardless of any keywords those units are normally required to have.  
**Restrictions:** Until the end of the turn, **OFFICER** models from your army cannot issue Orders. You can only use this Stratagem **once**.

### LOCKDOWN (1CP)

**Type:** Tempestus Boarding Regiment – Strategic Ploy Stratagem  
**When:** Your Movement phase  
**Target:** One **ASTRA MILITARUM** unit from your army that has not been selected to move this phase.  
**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, after your unit ends a move, you can select one **open Hatchway** it moved through during that move; **close that Hatchway**.

### FIRE-PATTERN HURRICANUS (1CP)

**Type:** Tempestus Boarding Regiment – Battle Tactic Stratagem  
**When:** Your Shooting phase, just after a **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** unit from your army has shot.  
**Target:** That **MILITARUM TEMPESTUS** unit.  
**Effect:** Select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks. Until the start of your next Shooting phase:

- subtract **2** from the **Move** characteristic of models in that enemy unit;
- subtract **2** from **Advance** rolls made for it;
- subtract **2** from **Charge** rolls made for it.
